# Privacy Policy for PixelDiet

**Last updated: March 15, 2026**

## Overview

PixelDiet is a fully offline image compression application for macOS. Your privacy is important to us.

## Data Collection

PixelDiet does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

- **No analytics** - No usage tracking, crash reporting, or telemetry
- **No network access** - The app makes zero network requests. All image processing happens locally on your Mac
- **No accounts** - No sign-in, registration, or user accounts required
- **No advertising** - No ads, no ad tracking, no ad identifiers
- **No third-party SDKs** - No external services or data processors

## Data Stored Locally

PixelDiet stores the following data on your device only:

- **User preferences** - Output format, quality, resize settings (stored in UserDefaults, never transmitted)
- **Folder bookmarks** - Security-scoped bookmarks for output folder access across launches (stored in UserDefaults)

This data never leaves your Mac and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Image Processing

All image compression, resizing, and format conversion is performed entirely on your Mac using Apple's native frameworks (ImageIO, CoreImage, vImage). Your images are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any server or third party.

## EXIF Metadata

PixelDiet can strip EXIF metadata (including GPS location data) from images during compression. This feature is enabled by default to protect your location privacy.

## Children's Privacy

PixelDiet does not collect any data from any users, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
